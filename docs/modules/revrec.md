# Revenue Recognition Module Plan

## Purpose

Generate simplified ASC 606-style revenue schedules and journal entries.

## Responsibilities

- Define performance obligations
- Generate revenue schedules from invoice and contract terms
- Split revenue into earned and deferred amounts
- Generate journal entries
- Support review and posting states

## Recognition Methods

- Immediate
- Straight-line over service period
- Usage-based
- Milestone-based

## Planned Entities

- `performance_obligations`
- `revenue_schedules`
- `journal_entries`

## MVP Simplifications

- No full ASC 606 compliance claim
- No ERP integration
- No complex contract combination logic
- No advanced standalone selling price allocation
